Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

Drumattic is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). Contact: dal001.dev@gmail.com.

Drumattic works on your own devices and needs no account. The only information that can leave your device is motion data you choose to share (see "Sharing motion data") and feedback you choose to email us.

What Drumattic uses, and why

  • Motion sensors (iPhone and Apple Watch). Used to detect when and where you hit, so the right drum plays. Motion is processed instantly on the device.
  • Bluetooth. Your Apple Watch and iPhone talk to each other directly over Bluetooth so the watch hand plays without delay. Only hit events and timing signals are exchanged, and only between your own devices.
  • Workout session on Apple Watch (Health). Drumattic starts a workout session only so the watch app keeps running while the screen is off. It does not read any Health data, and the session is discarded when you stop, so no workout is saved to Health.
  • Audio. Drumattic plays sound. It does not use the microphone.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings, chosen kit and personal kit positions.
  • Your practice history, streak and lesson progress.
  • Motion recordings from kit setup and Lab tests: how your phone and watch moved, with the timing of the test.

Sharing motion data (optional)

Sharing is off until you turn it on. If you turn on Share motion data (asked once after kit setup; you can change it anytime in Settings), Drumattic sends over Wi-Fi: - the motion recordings from kit setups and Lab tests, and their results (how many hits were detected and how accurately); - technical details: device models (iPhone and Apple Watch), operating-system versions (iOS, watchOS) and Drumattic app versions; - a random install ID created by the app, so recordings from the same install can be grouped and deleted on request.

We never send your name, email, Apple ID, contacts or location with this data. Like any internet connection, the upload reveals your IP address to the storage provider; we do not store it with the recordings or use it to identify you.

Why: to improve hit detection, accuracy and the performance of Drumattic, to develop new features, and for our own internal research, analysis and product development. We do not sell this data, share it with advertisers, or use it to identify you.

Legal basis: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by turning sharing off; nothing more is sent after that.

Where it is stored: with our cloud storage provider, Supabase, Inc. (servers may be outside your country). The provider stores the data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes.

How long: until it is no longer needed for the purposes above, and at most 5 years after upload, or until you ask us to delete it.

Feedback

If you send feedback, it is sent by email from your own mail app to dal001.dev@gmail.com and includes your email address, your message, the app version and device model, and — only if you leave it switched on — the app's diagnostic log (app events such as audio and connection status; no motion recordings). We use it to answer you and improve the app, and keep it as long as needed for that, at most 3 years.

What Drumattic does not do

  • No account, sign-in or personal profile.
  • No advertising, cross-app tracking or data sale.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

Your rights and deleting your data

  • On your device: Profile › Delete my data removes your history, progress, kit setup and recordings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Data you already shared: email dal001.dev@gmail.com with your install ID (shown in Settings) to see, delete or stop the use of it. We will answer within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or Korea under the Personal Information Protection Act), you also have the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

Drumattic does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information. If you are under 14, ask a parent or guardian before turning on Share motion data. If we learn that a child under 14 shared data without that permission, we will delete it.

Security

Uploads are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The storage only lets the app add new files; uploaded data can't be read back through the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new effective date. If we want to use shared data in a materially different way, we will ask for your consent again.
