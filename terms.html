Play safely

  • Hold your phone securely. A wrist strap or grip is recommended.
  • Keep a clear space around you, away from people, pets and breakable things.
  • Use small, relaxed motions. Stop if you feel pain, numbness or discomfort, and take regular breaks.
  • Keep the volume at a comfortable level, especially with earphones.
  • Children should play with a grown-up nearby.

Swinging a device carries a risk of dropping it or hitting something. Please play carefully; you are responsible for where and how you swing your devices.
